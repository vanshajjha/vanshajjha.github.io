# HR thought leaders to draw on

Vanshaj wants posts to engage with what prominent HR thinkers are saying. For each post, check this list for relevant recent work, and reference 1–3 of these people where they genuinely add to the argument.

## How to use them
- **Engage, don't echo.** Cite their idea, then add Vanshaj's angle: agree and extend, apply it to an Indian or tech-services context, or respectfully disagree. A post that only summarises others is not a point of view.
- **Paraphrase.** At most one short quote (under 15 words) per source. Never reproduce their frameworks or infographics wholesale.
- **Link to the original** and name the person and the piece.
- **LinkedIn blocks automated reading.** Prefer each person's own site, newsletter archive or published papers. If something exists only on LinkedIn and can't be read, don't cite it from memory. Leave a placeholder for Vanshaj: `> [CHECK: <person> wrote about this on LinkedIn — add link if relevant]`.
- **Don't imitate their writing style.** The voice stays Vanshaj's (see `style-guide.md`).
