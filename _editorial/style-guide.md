# Style guide

**Status: PROVISIONAL.** This is a starting default. It will be rewritten from Vanshaj's own writing samples (LinkedIn posts, past articles) and updated after each post from the edits he makes to drafts.

## Voice
- First person, conversational but sharp. A practitioner talking to peers, not a consultant presenting to a client.
- Confident opinions, honestly hedged where the evidence is thin.
- Indian business context by default (₹, lakh/crore where natural, Indian companies and labour market), global references where useful.

## Rhythm
- Short paragraphs (2–4 sentences). Vary sentence length; one short punchy sentence per section is fine.
- Plain words over jargon. When an HR term is needed, explain it in one clause.

## Structure habits
- Open with a scene, a number or a contradiction.
- Name the business problem before the HR one.
- Use one worked example the reader can follow end to end.
- Close with what the reader should do on Monday morning.
